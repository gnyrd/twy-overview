# How do I change someone's email address?

Two kinds of people get email from us, and the answer is different for each.

## A member

Ask HeyMarvelous support to change the address on the student's record (the HeyMarvelous admin screens will not let you edit it). That is the whole job. Within two nights the email system follows by itself: the member lists, the newsletter list and any class list move to the new address, an unsubscribe on the old address carries over, a welcome sequence in progress continues at the new address, and the old address stops receiving anything.

Why two nights: the change HeyMarvelous support makes reaches our copy of the student records the next morning, and the member lists are rebuilt from it the night after. Nothing to do on our side in between.

## Everyone else

People who are not members exist only in the email system. The person signs up again on the website with the new address and clicks unsubscribe in any email that reaches the old one. Nothing to do on our side either.

## What this will not do

- It does not change the address at HeyMarvelous. Support does that, on request.
- It does not merge two different people. A member's new address is recognised because HeyMarvelous still knows them as the same student.
- There is no page to edit a person's address by hand, on purpose. A hand edit in the email system was undone by the nightly rebuild, and this replaces that.
