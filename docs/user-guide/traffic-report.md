# What is the daily website traffic report in Slack?

## What it is

Every morning at 7:10 Mountain time a website traffic message is posted to the #status-traffic channel in Slack, right after the daily membership report. It says how many people visited the websites and where they came from, measured by Plausible, the privacy-friendly analytics on the sites (no cookies, no personal data).

## What it shows

- **Yesterday** on tiffanywoodyoga.com: visitors and pageviews, compared with the same weekday a week earlier; how many came from a search engine and how many from an AI tool (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, Copilot); the top three sources and the top three pages.
- **This month so far**: the same counts for the month to date, compared with the same number of days in the previous month, plus the previous month's full total.
- **This year so far**: visitors and pageviews for the year to date, compared with the same span a year earlier once a year of data exists (tracking on the main site started in 2026).
- **Other sites**: one line each for the studio (studio.tiffanywoodyoga.com, the HeyMarvelous site) and the Yoga Habit page.

Visitors are unique people for the period, pageviews are page loads. A visitor who comes twice in a month counts once in the month line and once on each day.

## What it will not do

- It does not show who the visitors are. Plausible does not know.
- It does not show Google search words. Those live in Google Search Console, which is a separate report.
- It does not count members watching classes inside the studio app; only page visits are measured.

## What you need to do

Nothing. It posts every day whether or not the numbers changed. If it is missing one morning, the system alerts JP.
