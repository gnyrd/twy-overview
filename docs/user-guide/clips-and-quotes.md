# How do Clips and Quotes get picked and posted?

Every class you teach becomes short pieces of social content without you doing anything: Clips (a short vertical video of you teaching, captioned) and Quotes (one line you said in class, on a card, with your own voice saying it). A person picks which ones go out. The schedule does the rest.

## Where to go

The Clips dashboard is at clips.tiffanywoodyoga.com (it signs in with a password). The front page lists recent classes. Each class page (/class) shows every candidate clip from that class with its caption, a thumbnail, and a captioned and an uncaptioned version. From there a clip can be added to the Clips Queue, marked as a Quote, added to the Cues queue, or downloaded. A custom clip can also be cut from any stretch of the class if the automatic picks missed the moment, and each class has a YouTube Opening (the first minutes of class, prepared for YouTube). A single clip has its own page (/clip) with a copyable link.

The queues each have a page: the Clips Queue (/ig/queue), the Quotes Queue (/quotes/queue), the Cues queue (/cues/queue), and the YouTube queue (/yt/queue). A queue is simply the list of things a person has approved. Nothing is ever posted that nobody clicked into a queue.

## How the clips come to exist

1. The Zoom recording downloads to the server within about 30 minutes of class ending.
2. The transcript is read to find the strongest short moments: a complete thought, a clear cue, a line worth hearing on its own. Each candidate gets a score.
3. Those moments are cut as vertical clips with captions burned in, plus a thumbnail. Captioned and uncaptioned versions are kept.
4. They appear on the class page, waiting to be picked. Unpicked clips are cleared out after about four weeks.

## Who picks

Today JP picks. Picking is looking through a class's clips and clicking the ones that feel right into the Clips Queue, and marking the lines worth a Quote. The queues are the only thing the schedulers read, so the eye that picks is the editorial control over everything that goes out.

## What gets posted, and when

All posting goes through Zernio, the scheduling service connected to the Instagram and Facebook accounts.

**Instagram**
- Since 2026-09-17 Instagram posts from the Inventory through its own publisher, the way Facebook and YouTube do, on the same timetable as before. The posts queued before the switch (through 2026-09-30) keep their slots and their captions.
- A Clip goes out about 24 hours before each class, picked from the approved Clips that have a caption ready: never a clip from one of the last few classes posted, never a segment Instagram has already used, a Habit slot preferring a Habit clip, and an item another service posted within seven days is skipped. Its caption names the coming class and carries the link to the Habit page.
- A Story goes out about 2 hours before each class with the class time and the same link.
- The free monthly Habit class also gets a Clip about a week ahead, because that is the post with a registration link worth clicking.
- Quotes go out four times a week, Tuesday 12:30, Thursday 12:30, Friday 7:00 and Sunday 8:30 Mountain, from the Quotes Queue, oldest first, never the same words twice on this account. Two of the four slots each week take a still card and two take a Reel (the card with your voice from the class), which slots get which flips each week, and Instagram takes the opposite format from Facebook on the same slot, so the two never carry the same format at the same hour. A Reel slot needs a quote with voice audio, and one without is held rather than posted silent.
- If a class is cancelled after its posts were queued, the Clip and the Story for it are taken down before they post. A class published late, after its posting times have passed, gets its last missed post a few minutes later.
- Labs. Since 2026-09-18 a regular class Clip goes out as a trial Reel: Instagram shows it to people who do not follow the account first, and shares it with followers on its own once it performs, so a clip reaches strangers by design instead of the same 2% of followers. Its caption ends on one ask chosen by what the clip is (a teaching or meditation clip: Save this for your next practice. Anything else: Send this to someone who needs it today.), and the upcoming class line with the free Habit class invitation sits in the post's first comment, which the account writes itself the moment the Reel publishes. The Habit week Reel and the Stories are unchanged. If Instagram refuses the trial, the Reel still posts as a normal one and the Inventory records that. Exploratory: with the switch off, Reels post to everyone as before, with the class line in the caption and no first comment.
- Labs. Since 2026-09-18 a still quote card posts as a two picture carousel: the quote card, then a second card in the same colours naming the class the words came from and making one ask (Send this to someone who needs it today, or for words from a teaching or meditation segment, Save this for your next practice). Instagram shows carousels to more people than single pictures and shows an unswiped one again from its second picture. The quote's caption, still or Reel, ends on the same ask, and the free Habit class invitation sits in the post's first comment whenever a future Habit class is planned. The weekly growth review now reads follows and profile visits per still post from Instagram itself (Reels cannot report them). Exploratory: with the switch off, a quote posts as one picture with the invitation in the caption.
- Labs. Since 2026-09-18 anyone who comments HABIT on an Instagram or Facebook post gets the free Yoga Habit class link by direct message within seconds, with a short public reply under their comment (Sent, check your messages). Class Clips and Quotes say so in their caption (Comment HABIT and the link comes to your messages). It works on any post, not only new ones, one message per comment, and it saves no address: the person joins the list by registering on the page the link opens, and those signups are counted as coming from a message. The weekly growth review shows, per platform, how many comments matched, messages sent and failed, people reached and link clicks. Exploratory: with the switch off, the messages stop and the caption line goes.
- An Instagram post that does not publish is noticed the same way a Facebook post or a Short is: a check every 20 minutes, a note to Slack when a post failed or is still waiting 45 minutes after its slot, and one retry from the saved copy.
- The weekly audience review compares the Reels and the photo posts as groups (how many, median reach, likes, saves, shares) so the format question gets answered with your own numbers. Carousels (several cards in one post) are not built.
- Every quote post also records the card look it used (background colour, type treatment, accent) from the approved set, on Instagram and Facebook alike, and the weekly review lists results by look, so over time the looks that travel best can be found. Looks are drawn at random per post; a Facebook series can still pin one look.

**YouTube**
- Labs. Posting to YouTube is exploratory as a whole: with the switch off, no Short is scheduled, the publish checks and the channel's numbers stop, and the Stats pages show Instagram only. Nothing already published is touched.
- Since 2026-09-17 YouTube follows the same timetable as Instagram. A Short goes out 24 hours before each class Instagram posts for, a week before the free monthly Habit class, and at the quote slots that take a Reel (two of the four a week), so a Quote with your voice becomes a Short too. Every Short comes from the Inventory: for a class slot the highest-scoring approved Clip (a Habit slot prefers a Habit clip, never two from the same class in a week, never the same clip twice, and clips Instagram already posted only after the queued ones), for a quote slot the oldest approved Quote with voice audio, never the same words twice. The title is the clip's own first thought, the description quotes the line, names the class and date, and invites people onto the list for the class details. The link is the short habit.tiffanywoodyoga.com/yt/<class>-<clip> address (a Quote's ends in -quote), so the stats count visits per Short.
- The publisher keeps its own copy of every clip it posts, so a clip cleared from its class folder can still be posted again if YouTube rejects it. A wording change to the description reaches every Short still queued within half an hour. A Short already published keeps what YouTube has.
- Labs. Since 2026-09-18 every clip Short carries the subscribe ask in the video itself, low on the frame, in the same colours and lettering as the quote cards: a one-line strip (Subscribe. New clips, all week long) for the whole clip, and for the last three seconds a larger card with the same words that takes the strip's place, because nine in ten viewers leave before the end. It shows wherever the Short plays. Quote Shorts are already a card and do not get the overlay. The words live in one place in the code, so changing them changes every Short posted after the change; a Short already published keeps its overlay as posted.
- Labs. Every Short's description opens with the same ask (Subscribe. New clips from class, all week long.) and every Short, clip or quote, gets a pinned comment the moment it publishes: the ask, then the free monthly Yoga Habit class and its link, which is clickable there and nowhere on the Short itself. A wording change reaches queued Shorts within half an hour, comment included. Exploratory: with the switch off, Shorts upload as rendered and carry no comment.
- A Short that does not publish is noticed. Every 20 minutes a check reads each Short whose time has passed, and if it failed or is still waiting 45 minutes after its slot, a note goes to Slack. A Short that Zernio reports as failed on two separate checks is re-created once, from the publisher's copy, a few minutes later. If that copy is gone, nothing is re-posted and Slack says so. A published Short is never touched.
- Each Short's views, likes and comments are kept per month (since 2026-09-15, read from YouTube every four hours), the way Instagram and Facebook posts are. On the Stats site's Social page a Short sits in the same Best on record and Latest posts tables as the Instagram and Facebook posts, tagged YouTube, and the weekly growth review lists the week's Shorts and the subscriber count. YouTube reports no reach, so a Short's engagement is likes plus comments per hundred views. Since 2026-09-16 Studio's own numbers for each Short (who stayed to watch, the average view length, watch minutes, shares, subscribers gained, and where the views came from) show in a YouTube Studio table on the Social page and as extra columns in the weekly review's Shorts table, once YouTube reports them, about two days after a Short publishes. Until then those cells read Not enough data.

## What this will not do

- It will not post anything nobody picked. An empty queue means nothing goes out, and the daily reports will show the gap.
- Labs. Cues are a review list only. A Cue can be added to its queue and looked at, but nothing posts a Cue anywhere yet. Exploratory: the Cues queue and its button carry the Labs badge (hover it for the one line that says so).
- A queued Quote's words cannot be changed (since 2026-09-16). The Quotes Queue shows each quote as it will post. To change the wording, remove the Quote and add a new one from the class page.
- The YouTube queue of class openings is a list only: nothing uploads an opening, that is still a manual step.
- The WhatsApp queue is a list, nothing sends to WhatsApp from it.
- Once a post is up, this system cannot take it down. Removing a published Facebook or Instagram post is done on the Page or the app itself.

## If something looks wrong

A post that should have gone out and did not, a clip with the wrong caption, or a Quote that sounds off: tell JP. The daily Slack reports carry the posting counts, so a quiet week is visible without anyone checking the dashboard.
