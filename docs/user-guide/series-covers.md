# How does a Series get its cover photo?

Trial. On the 1st of each month, right after the new Series is created in the On-demand Library, the system looks for a photo of that month's apex pose and sets it as the Series cover. You do nothing unless the email below arrives.

## Where the photo comes from

- The 2022 studio shoot in the TWY Google Drive (Photo Shoots, Tiff 2022 Studio, web).
- Only photos that carry the Yoga label in Drive count, and only when the label names one of the poses in the month's **Apex pose** line of the Monthly Overview. Poses are tried in reverse order: the last one named first, then the one before it.
- Labels marked **Needs check** are never used. **Confirmed** ones come first, then **Auto**.
- A photo that has been a Series cover in the last 15 months is skipped, whether the system set it or you did by hand.
- When several photos qualify, the last one from the shoot wins.

## When nothing fits

One email goes to admin@ and tiffany@ saying which Series has no cover, which poses were tried and why each came up empty (not in the label vocabulary, no labeled photo, or all used recently). The Series stays without a cover until someone sets one in HeyMarvelous (the Series card on the product's Content tab, Settings). The system does not try again that month.

## What this will not do

- It never replaces a cover that is already there, on any Series.
- It reads the Drive photos and their labels; it does not move, rename, relabel or delete anything in Drive.
- It does not change the Series name, its classes or its order.
- With the switch off, the 1st-of-month step creates the Series and asks JP for a cover, exactly as before.
