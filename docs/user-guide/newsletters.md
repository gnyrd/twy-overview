# How do newsletters work?

## Where to go

Open [https://classes.tiffanywoodyoga.com](https://classes.tiffanywoodyoga.com), or click **Emails** in the toolbar above the class calendar. From there, click **Monthly Drafts** to open the timeline for the most recent month with drafts, one row per audience, in the order it will send. Links beside the month name step to the closest earlier or later month that has drafts.

## The eight emails

A month can carry up to eight emails, one row per audience:

- **Yoga Lifestyle: Monthly** -- the regular newsletter to current members.
- **Yoga Habit: General Invitation** -- the free class invitation to everyone else.
- **Yoga Habit: Resend** -- a second try at the invitation, to people who did not open it.
- **Yoga Habit: Gentle Reminder** -- a nudge to people who opened the invitation but have not registered.
- **Yoga Habit: Registered Reminder** -- a reminder to people who did register.
- **Yoga Habit: Class Recording** -- points everyone who registered to the recording.
- **Yoga Habit: Follow Up 1** -- the first note after the class.
- **Yoga Habit: Follow Up 2** -- the second note, a week later.

Not every month uses all eight. A row only appears once that email has a real send date to anchor to. Draft rows for next month start appearing on the timeline on or after the 25th.

## Editing a draft

Click into any row to open it. Each one has a subject, a preview line (the snippet an inbox shows next to the subject), and the body, all edited right on the page.

## Marking a draft ready

Tick **Ready to Send** and save. That is your sign-off that the copy is final. A draft moves through four states as it goes: **Draft** while you are still writing it, **Ready to Send** once you tick the box, **Scheduled** once it is queued for its send time, and **Sent** once it has gone out.

## Turning a send on or off

Every draft shows its scheduled date and time next to a **Scheduled** toggle. Switch it off to hold that one email back, or on to let it go out at its scheduled time. The rest of the timeline is not affected either way.

## Names and links that fill in on their own

A draft can reference details that are not fixed until later in the month, like the class title or the link to the class recording. Those fill in automatically from that month's real facts right before the email sends. If one of them is not ready yet, for example the recording link before the recording is posted, that email waits rather than going out with a placeholder still showing.

## After it sends

Delivery numbers (sent, opened, clicked) appear on the same row once an email has gone out.

## What this will not do

There is no Slack step. Nothing sends from a draft that is not marked Ready to Send. And editing a draft after its send has already gone out does not change what people already received.

## What you need to do

Write and edit each month's emails, tick Ready to Send when the copy is final, and use the Scheduled toggle if you need to hold one back. Timing, fill-in details, and delivery numbers are automatic.
