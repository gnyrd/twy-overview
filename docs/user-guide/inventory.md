# What is the Inventory?

The Inventory is one list of every Clip, Quote and Cue a person has ever approved on the Clips dashboard, together with where each one went: which service posted it (Instagram, Facebook, YouTube), when, and whether it published. It is the record behind the queues. The queues are what is still waiting; the Inventory is that plus everything that has already run.

## Where to go

On the Clips dashboard (clips.tiffanywoodyoga.com), the Inventory button sits beside the three queue buttons at the top of every page, and the page itself is /inventory. It opens on the items still in a queue. A switch at the top shows History (items that have already gone out and left the queues) or All, another narrows to Clips, Quotes or Cues, and a search box finds an item by class, clip name or the words of a quote.

## What each line shows

- The item: its kind, its score, the class it came from and the clip name, which links back to that clip on the class page. A Quote also shows its words.
- In queue, when a person's queue still holds it.
- Every placement: the service that posted it (for example ig_reel for an Instagram class Reel, fb_photo for a Facebook quote card, yt_short for a YouTube Short), the date and time it was scheduled for, and its state: published, scheduled, failed, withdrawn (pulled before it posted, usually because the class was cancelled) or cancelled (replaced by another post).
- A verdict, once a service has said it is done with the item: placed, or rejected.

The line above the list gives the totals: how many items are in a queue, how many are history only, and how many placements are published, scheduled, failed, or withdrawn. It also says when the Inventory was last refreshed.

## Where the information comes from

Every 30 minutes the Inventory asks Zernio (the scheduling service) whether each post due within the hour has published, so a post's state follows what Zernio reports. Since 2026-09-17 the queues are part of the Inventory itself: approving a Clip, a Quote or a Cue on the Clips dashboard writes it here at once, and each publisher (Instagram, Facebook, YouTube) records every post here the moment it is scheduled, so they show at once. Items that went out before the Inventory existed (from 2026-05 on) were read in from the posting records, so their history is complete back to the first Instagram post, and a few early Quotes that were recorded by their words alone are listed under Older quotes.

## What this will not do

- It is read only. Nothing on this page adds, removes, posts or takes anything down. The queues stay the place to approve a clip or a quote, and the class pages stay the place to pick one.
- Since 2026-09-17 all three services post from the Inventory: each publisher picks from the items in a queue here, as the Clips and Quotes page describes. The Instagram posts queued by the old scheduler before the switch (through 2026-09-30) keep their slots and captions and are listed here as its posts.
- A placement that was withdrawn before it posted (a cancelled class, a post taken down) does not use the item up. The item stays available to be picked again on any of the three services.
- An item that has already run and whose class folder has been cleared has no video left to post. It stays in the Inventory as the record of what went where, and is never a candidate again.
- A queued Quote's words cannot be changed. To change the wording, remove the Quote and add a new one (since 2026-09-16).

## If something looks wrong

A post you know went out that shows as scheduled, an item in a queue that is missing here, or a count that does not match the queue buttons: tell JP. The Inventory checks itself against the queues and posting records on every refresh and reports a mismatch to Slack, so a difference is already known about or is new information.
