# How do I cancel a class?

## Where to go

Open the class on [https://classes.tiffanywoodyoga.com](https://classes.tiffanywoodyoga.com) by clicking its day on the calendar. At the bottom of the page, next to Save, is **Cancel class**. It is only offered for a class that is still ahead and already has its HeyMarvelous event.

## What happens when you confirm

A dialog names the class, its day and time, and asks once. **Cancel class** on the right does it. **Keep class** on the left does nothing. On confirm, HeyMarvelous marks its event cancelled first, then the plan here. If HeyMarvelous refuses, nothing changes and the page tells you.

## What stops

- The class shows as cancelled on the calendar here, with a red Cancelled capsule, and the plan page carries a Cancelled banner saying when and where it was cancelled.
- The subscribed calendar feeds mark the entry cancelled. Most calendar apps strike it through or hide it, instead of the class dropping out silently.
- No Instagram story or clip posts for it, and one already queued is withdrawn.
- No recording is expected for it, so nobody is asked where it is.
- The Habit page and the newsletter tokens skip a cancelled class when they look for the next one.

## Telling people

Under the banner is **Write the cancellation email**. It opens a new newsletter with the title, subject and three plain lines already filled in: which class and when, that it is cancelled, and the next class of that type when there is one within two weeks. You pick the audience, change anything you like, and send it the way you send any newsletter. Nothing is sent by itself.

## Bringing it back

**Restore class** on a cancelled plan reverses it, with the same kind of confirmation: HeyMarvelous first, then the plan, and posting resumes. A class whose event was deleted in HeyMarvelous cannot be restored from here, and the banner says so.

## Cancelling in HeyMarvelous

That works too. Every hour the system reads the coming two weeks of classes from HeyMarvelous and brings a cancellation, or a restore, across to the plan here, with a note in Slack saying it came from HeyMarvelous.

## What this will not do

- It never emails anyone by itself.
- It does not touch a class that has already started, or any class in the past.
- It does not change the Zoom meeting or anything on Facebook.
- It does not delete the plan. Everything you wrote stays.
