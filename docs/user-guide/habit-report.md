# How did the Yoga Habit class do?

A week after each Yoga Habit class, at 10:00 in the morning, a short report appears in the #updates Slack channel, and JP forwards it to you as a direct message, the same way as the Kula Hello.

It has a small table and three lines.

The table puts the class next to the two before it: how many people visited the Habit page, how many registered and how many of those were not yet members, how many came live (again with the non-members), how many took the recording, and how many non-members have joined The Yoga Lifestyle since. Joining is counted for three weeks after the class, so a week on it says "so far".

The three lines are written by Claude from those numbers, the emails for that class, and the calendar around the date: the biggest change and its most likely cause, the second thing worth knowing, and one thing to try for the next class. When a line is a guess rather than a number, it says so ("my read is").

## What it will NOT do

- It never changes anything. It only reports.
- It does not name anyone. The numbers are counts.
- The email numbers start in September 2026, so the earliest reports compare emails only from then on.
- The first report is for the October 10, 2026 class, on October 17, 2026.
