# How does Features? work?

**Features?** lists things that could be built for the studio. It is at [classes.tiffanywoodyoga.com/features](https://classes.tiffanywoodyoga.com/features), and the Features? button in the column on the right edge of the Classes calendar opens it.

Every item says the same four things, so two of them can be compared at a glance:

- **Title**, what it is called.
- **Description**, what it does.
- **Benefit**, what it changes for the studio.
- **Estimated hours**, how long it would take.

## Waiting on you

Open an item and you have two buttons:

- **Yes**: it gets built, and you will hear when it is running.
- **Not now**: it moves down to **Later** and stays there.

There is no No. Anything under Later can be said yes to at any time, and an answer you already gave can be changed.

## Your notes

Every item has a notes box, reachable from the list or from the item itself. A note is saved exactly as you wrote it and stays that way, so nobody can quietly reword what you said. To add to it or change your mind, write another note.

## Asking for something

**Ask**, in the top right, takes what you want in your own words. Your item then sits at the top of the page waiting on an estimate, and comes back to you with the same four things and the same two buttons as everything else.

## What it will not do

- Saying yes is not a bill. It is the go-ahead to build; the hours are invoiced the way they always are.
- Nothing on this page sends an email, changes a class or touches the website. It is a list of decisions, not a set of switches.
- Items set aside are never cleaned out.
