# How do Clips and Quotes get picked and posted?

Every class you teach becomes short pieces of social content without you doing anything: Clips (a short vertical video of you teaching, captioned) and Quotes (one line you said in class, on a card, with your own voice saying it). A person picks which ones go out. The schedule does the rest.

## Where to go

The Clips dashboard is at clips.tiffanywoodyoga.com (it signs in with a password). The front page lists recent classes. Each class page (/class) shows every candidate clip from that class with its caption, a thumbnail, and a captioned and an uncaptioned version. From there a clip can be added to the Clips Queue, marked as a Quote, added to the Cues queue, or downloaded. A custom clip can also be cut from any stretch of the class if the automatic picks missed the moment, and each class has a YouTube Opening (the first minutes of class, prepared for YouTube). A single clip has its own page (/clip) with a copyable link.

The queues each have a page: the Clips Queue (/ig/queue), the Quotes Queue (/quotes/queue), the Cues queue (/cues/queue), and the YouTube queue (/yt/queue). A queue is simply the list of things a person has approved. Nothing is ever posted that nobody clicked into a queue.

## How the clips come to exist

1. The Zoom recording downloads to the server within about 30 minutes of class ending.
2. The transcript is read to find the strongest short moments: a complete thought, a clear cue, a line worth hearing on its own. Each candidate gets a score.
3. Those moments are cut as vertical clips with captions burned in, plus a thumbnail. Captioned and uncaptioned versions are kept.
4. They appear on the class page, waiting to be picked. Unpicked clips are cleared out after about four weeks.

## Who picks

Today JP picks. Picking is looking through a class's clips and clicking the ones that feel right into the Clips Queue, and marking the lines worth a Quote. The queues are the only thing the schedulers read, so the eye that picks is the editorial control over everything that goes out.

## What gets posted, and when

All posting goes through Zernio, the scheduling service connected to the Instagram and Facebook accounts.

**Instagram**
- A Clip goes out about 24 hours before each class, drawn from the Clips Queue, and its caption names the coming class and carries the link to the Habit page.
- A Story goes out about 2 hours before each class with the class time and the same link.
- The free monthly Habit class also gets a Clip about a week ahead, because that is the post with a registration link worth clicking.
- Quotes go out four times a week, Tuesday 12:30, Thursday 12:30, Friday 7:00 and Sunday 8:30 Mountain, drawn from the Quotes Queue. Each quote posts once. Since 2026-09-07 a quote is either a Reel (the card with your voice from the class) or a photo post (the same style of card, still, no sound): two of the four slots take one format and two the other, and which slots get which flips each week, so no format sits on the same day for long. A Reel whose audio cannot be found is held rather than posted silent.
- The weekly audience review compares the Reels and the photo posts as groups (how many, median reach, likes, saves, shares) so the format question gets answered with your own numbers. Carousels (several cards in one post) are not built.
- Every quote post also records the card look it used (background colour, type treatment, accent) from the approved set, on Instagram and Facebook alike, and the weekly review lists results by look, so over time the looks that travel best can be found. Looks are drawn at random per post; a Facebook series can still pin one look.

**Facebook**
- Three Clips a week, Tuesday 9:00, Thursday 9:00 and Friday 7:00 Mountain. Each one promotes the next class after that slot, and in a Habit week one of the three promotes the Habit class instead.
- Facebook takes the highest-scoring approved clip; Instagram picks at random among the approved ones. That difference is deliberate.
- Quotes are back on Facebook from 2026-09-07: four a week, Monday 12:30, Wednesday 12:30, Saturday 9:00 and Sunday 12:30 Mountain, from the same Quotes Queue as Instagram, so one quote can appear on both. Two of the four are Reels with your voice and two are photo cards, and which slots get which flips each week, the same format test as Instagram. A Reel slot takes the next quote that has voice audio; a photo slot takes the next quote. The weekly review compares the Facebook Reels and photo cards as groups too.

## What this will not do

- It will not post anything nobody picked. An empty queue means nothing goes out, and the daily reports will show the gap.
- Cues are a review list only. A Cue can be added to its queue and looked at, but nothing posts a Cue anywhere yet.
- The YouTube queue holds the openings a person has chosen, but nothing uploads them. Putting them on YouTube is still a manual step.
- The WhatsApp queue is a list, nothing sends to WhatsApp from it.
- Once a post is up, this system cannot take it down. Removing a published Facebook or Instagram post is done on the Page or the app itself.

## If something looks wrong

A post that should have gone out and did not, a clip with the wrong caption, or a Quote that sounds off: tell JP. The daily Slack reports carry the posting counts, so a quiet week is visible without anyone checking the dashboard.
