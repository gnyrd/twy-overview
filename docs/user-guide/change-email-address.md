# How do I change someone's email address?

Two kinds of people get email from us, and the answer is different for each.

## Someone HeyMarvelous knows

If the person has ever bought, enrolled or registered through HeyMarvelous (a member now or in the past, a class pass buyer, a Yoga Habit registrant), ask HeyMarvelous support to change the address on their student record (the HeyMarvelous admin screens will not let you edit it). That is the whole job. Within two nights the email system follows by itself: every list the old address was on moves to the new address, an unsubscribe on the old address carries over, a welcome sequence in progress continues at the new address, and the old address stops receiving anything.

Why two nights: the change HeyMarvelous support makes reaches our copy of the student records the next morning, and the email system reads that copy the night after. Nothing to do on our side in between.

## Everyone else

People HeyMarvelous has never seen, which means newsletter signups from the website, exist only in the email system. The person signs up again on the website with the new address and clicks unsubscribe in any email that reaches the old one. Nothing to do on our side either.

## What this will not do

- It does not change the address at HeyMarvelous. Support does that, on request.
- It does not merge two different people. A new address is recognised because HeyMarvelous still knows the person as the same student.
- It never adds someone to the email system just because HeyMarvelous knows them. Only people who already receive email are followed.
- There is no page to edit a person's address by hand, on purpose. A hand edit in the email system was undone by the nightly rebuild, and this replaces that.
